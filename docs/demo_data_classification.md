# Synthetic data classification standard

Fictional test standard, not Superhuman policy. Version: 1.0. Policy updated: 2026-10-30.

## Public
Public data may be shared externally after publication approval. A draft intended for publication is not automatically Public. A fictional example is an approved, published product statistics report.

## Internal
Internal data is intended for authenticated employees only. Do not publish it externally without an approved classification change and publication approval. A fictional example is an internal engineering operations guide with no personal records or secrets.

## Restricted
Restricted data requires explicit approval from its designated data owner. Personal customer records and authentication secrets are fictional examples of Restricted content. Never paste these records or secrets into this chatbot. Sensitive source data must not be placed in the broadly shared policy ingestion folder.

## Derived datasets
A derived dataset inherits the most restrictive classification of its inputs until the owner reviews and approves a different classification. Aggregation alone does not justify lowering a classification. Unclassified data must not be treated as Public; request owner review before sharing.

## Classification changes
The designated owner reviews classification changes and records the decision in the live catalog. A change to Public also requires publication approval. This standard explains labels; it does not establish the current classification of any particular table, job, or dashboard.

## Retention is separate
A classification label does not set a retention duration. Consult the dataset's approved retention policy and any applicable hold.
