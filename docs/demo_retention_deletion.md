# Synthetic retention and deletion procedure

Fictional test procedure, not Superhuman policy. Version: 1.0. Policy updated: 2026-10-30.

## Choosing a retention period
Every dataset needs an owner-approved retention schedule that states its purpose, duration, and disposal method. Public, Internal, and Restricted labels do not independently determine retention. This document intentionally provides no universal retention duration and no retention assignment for individual assets.

## Holds
An applicable legal or investigation hold suspends routine deletion. The designated owner must consult the authorized Legal or Privacy team before approving disposal when a hold may apply. Do not infer hold status from a document's age or classification.

## Deprecation checklist
Before deprecating a dataset, inspect the live lineage catalog, identify downstream consumers, contact their owners, and record a migration plan. The current dependencies and asset owner must come from live systems; this procedure contains no current asset assignments.

## Deletion approval
The owner must review downstream dependencies before approving deletion. Verify the retention schedule and hold status before execution. Protected datasets cannot be deleted through the bot. This Q&A prototype cannot execute deletion, alter retention, or modify any dataset.

## Verification
An authorized operator verifies the actual system state after an approved deletion and records the outcome in the audit system. A chatbot response is not evidence that deletion occurred.
